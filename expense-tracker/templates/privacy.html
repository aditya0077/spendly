Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have over your data. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the Service.

We do not collect payment information. Spendly is a free tool and does not process transactions.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To display your expenses and summaries back to you.
  • To authenticate your account securely.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the user experience.

We will never sell your data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We may use limited third-party services (such as hosting providers) solely to operate the Service. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data for any other purpose.

We use Google Fonts to load typefaces. This may result in your browser making a request to Google's servers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle such requests.

6. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies to keep you logged in between page visits. These are essential for the Service to function and are not used for tracking or advertising purposes. No third-party cookies are set.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our site.
